# VANGUARD Engineering Record

Objective engineering record of VANGUARD development, experiments, evidence, failures, and verified decisions.

## Purpose

This repository preserves provenance and engineering history across the VANGUARD repositories.

It records:
- decisions and their evidence
- experiments and results
- failures and their conditions
- confirmed facts
- potential errors
- verification references

## Governing principle

> Probability may enter the loop, but it does not leave the loop as truth without confirmation.

A failed experiment is evidence. It is not automatically discarded and it is not automatically promoted to truth.
